| Audience | Model | Platform | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| US, 25–45: working adults with jobs, families, bills and goals | B2C: sell to people, not companies | Web only, runs in the browser, no mobile apps | 24 hours, idea to working product |

## How we are judged

| Weight | Criterion | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **40%** | Does it work? | Works end-to-end in production. Core value is real, not faked or hardcoded. UX and code quality: they read the repo. |
| 20% | What do we sell? | Clear what the user pays for and why they come back tomorrow. |
| 15% | Who do we sell to? | A clear problem and deep understanding of the target user. |
| 15% | Why does it make money? | Big or growing niche, $100M ARR long-term, LTV > CAC. |
| 10% | Is it new? | Not a copy-paste of an existing business. |

**Pass/fail:** B2C only (no B2B) · web only (no mobile apps) · all 3 submission items.
