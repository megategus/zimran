# Business Cup notes (from last year's Zimran workshop)

## Evaluation guidelines
1. **Competitor analysis**: what they run, what works for them, what applies to us.
2. **Monetization**: how we make money (subscription, freemium, one-time), pricing and why this price.
3. **User acquisition**: channels, clear funnel assumptions in numbers.
4. **Unit economics**: show the math. LTV and CAC explained, realistic numbers. **Excel with calculations is mandatory**; key numbers must also be in the deck and understandable without Excel.

## Formulas and rules of thumb
- CAC = total ad spend / paying users
- LTV = avg revenue per user x avg subscription length
- ROI = LTV / CAC - 1 (positive = earning)
- LTV/CAC 1-3+ (by niche and scale) = healthy; < 1 = losing money on every user
- CTR > 1% = good attention; CR > 2-3% = good campaign
- CTR < 1%: new creative. CTR > 1% and CR < 2%: fix the landing page. CR > 2% and CAC > LTV: raise price or cut CAC.

## Monetization
- Subscription or freemium are easiest to model for the Cup.
- Pricing is a signal: anchor yearly next to monthly (e.g. $9.99/mo vs $79.99/yr), free trial lowers friction.

## User card (answer before building)
Example persona: John, 42, Austin TX, office worker, wants practical AI skills.
- Core pain or desire?
- Where do they spend time online?
- What makes them stop scrolling?
- What objection stops them from buying?
- What does success look like?

## Pitch pitfalls
- Solution is hard to understand.
- Too many details, main idea lost.
- Solution does not answer a real problem.
- Good product, weak delivery.

Test: would a person who hears about the product for the first time understand what problem it solves and why it works?
